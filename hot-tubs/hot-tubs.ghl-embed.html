Coverage

What is actually warrantied

The full manufacturer warranty, in plain numbers. Register within 30 days of delivery and it applies to you as the original owner.

10YR

Shell structure

Structurally sound and free from water loss. The part that would end the whole thing if it failed.

3YR

Plumbing

All internal plumbing, wall fittings, jet fittings and bonded components including the filter compartment.

3YR

Acrylic surface

Against blistering or cracking caused by a defect in materials. Normal fading is not included.

3YR

Electrical equipment

Control systems, pumps and heaters against defect or malfunction. One year on lighting, audio and air controls.

Skirting panels carry a one year warranty. Coverage applies to the original purchaser under normal use and care. Ask us for the full terms at your appointment.
