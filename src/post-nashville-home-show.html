What the next hour looks like

Step one

Tell us about the yard

Where it goes, how we get it back there, what the surface is, and what power you have. Photos on your phone are plenty. This is the part that prevents surprises on delivery day.

Get in the water

Shoes off, climb in, try the seats. Check where the jets hit your shoulders and whether the lounger fits your height. For a pool, we walk the yard with you and sketch it in place.

Out-the-door price with your Home Show credit applied, what the pad or site needs, a delivery or build window, and financing if you want it. Written down. Yours to take home.
